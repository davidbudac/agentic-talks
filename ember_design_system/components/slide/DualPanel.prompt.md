The signature compare block: one cream panel and one ink panel side by side, for tradeoffs and contrasts.

```jsx
<DualPanel
  left={{ label: "YOU PAY", body: "Every subagent re-pays a fixed cost — its own system prompt and setup." }}
  right={{ label: "YOU WIN WHEN", body: "Isolated work would otherwise pollute the lead enough to degrade reasoning." }}
/>
```

- Left defaults to cream, right to ink — the warm/serious pairing. Override per-panel with `tone`.
- Keep both panels to one short idea each; this is a contrast, not a feature list.
