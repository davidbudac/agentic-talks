A single headline metric — a large Space Grotesk value over a quiet label. Stack two for a contrast (e.g. 5 min vs 1 hr).

```jsx
<StatTile value="5 min" label="default · sliding, resets on every read" accent />
<StatTile value="1 hr" label="optional · different price point" tone="light" />
```

- `accent` makes the value coral — use it for the primary/default figure.
- Keep labels to one line; this is a number with a caption, not a card of prose.
