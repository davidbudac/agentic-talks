Add currency column to CSV export

Finance imports the export into a spreadsheet that mixes EUR, CZK, GBP
and USD invoices, so the gross column was ambiguous without a currency.

CsvExport now writes a sixth column, currency, after gross. The value is
the invoice's normalised ISO code, so invoices without a currency export
EUR. The export contract Javadoc is updated to match.

Tests: update the expected header and rows in CsvExportTest and CliTest,
add a test for upper-casing and the EUR default, and add a round-trip
test that re-imports the export through FixtureLoader and checks that
the currency survives.
