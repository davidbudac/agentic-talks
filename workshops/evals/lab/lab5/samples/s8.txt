Add currency column to CSV export

Consumers of the export could not tell which currency a gross amount
was in, because the file mixes EUR, CZK, GBP and USD invoices.

Write the invoice's currency as a sixth column after gross, in upper
case, with EUR for invoices that have none. The header gains the
column; order, quoting and the gross rule are unchanged. Tests cover
the new column and the default.
