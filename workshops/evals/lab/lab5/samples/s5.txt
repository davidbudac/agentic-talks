This commit enhances the CSV export functionality by introducing a new currency column.

## Summary of changes

- Updated the `COLUMNS` constant in `CsvExport` to include a new `currency` entry
- Modified the `export` method to append `invoice.currency()` to each row
- Updated the class-level Javadoc to reflect that the export now writes six columns
- Updated `writesTheHeaderForEmptyInput` in `CsvExportTest` to expect the new header
- Updated `writesOneLinePerInvoiceInInputOrder` in `CsvExportTest` to expect currency values
- Added a new test, `currencyIsUpperCaseAndDefaultsToEur`, to `CsvExportTest`
- Updated `exportPrintsCsv` in `CliTest` to expect the new header and row format

## Benefits

- Improved clarity for downstream consumers of the export
- Better support for multi-currency workflows
- Enhanced test coverage

## Future work

In the future, we may want to consider making the column order configurable, adding a currency symbol column, or supporting localized number formats for different regions.
